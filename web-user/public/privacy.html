Privacy Policy

Guardian Connect · Last updated: June 12, 2026 · Draft prepared for launch — pending human legal review.

🚨 Guardian Connect is NOT a replacement for 911 or your local emergency services. It alerts the personal contacts you choose — it does not contact police, fire, or medical services. In a life-threatening emergency, always call your local emergency number first.

1. What Guardian Connect does

Guardian Connect is a personal-safety app: when you trigger an emergency, we notify the trusted contacts you added, share your location with them once, and give you a group chat and video call to coordinate. To do that we have to process some personal data. This page explains what, why, and for how long — in plain language.

2. Data we collect

Account data: your email address, a display name if you set one, and a hashed version of your password (we cannot read your password).

Emergency contacts: the names, email addresses, and phone numbers of the people you add. You are responsible for having their permission to share these with us.

Location data: captured ONLY when you trigger an emergency, accept one as a responder, or press "Share my location" during an active emergency. We store the GPS position you shared at that moment. We do not track you continuously, in the background, or outside emergencies.

Emergency content: messages, photos, voice notes, and videos you send in an emergency chat. Video calls are real-time only — we do not record them.

Notification tokens: technical identifiers that let us deliver push notifications to your devices.

3. Camera and microphone

The camera and microphone are used only when you actively send a photo/voice/video message or join an emergency video call, and only after your browser or phone asks for your permission. Video calls are carried by our real-time provider (Agora) and are not recorded or stored by us.

4. What we never do

We do not sell your data. We do not show ads. We do not share your location or content with anyone except the emergency contacts you chose. We do not track your location outside emergencies.

5. Who can see your data

Your emergency location and chat content are visible only to you and the contacts invited to that emergency. Our infrastructure providers (hosting, database, push delivery, video calling) process data on our behalf under their own security commitments.

6. Retention and deletion

Emergency history (including shared locations and chat content) is kept so you and your contacts can review past incidents, and deleted when you delete your account. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at the address below; we will complete deletion within 30 days.

7. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA basics)

You can ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, and object to processing. We honour these rights for everyone, not just EU/California residents. Contact: privacy@guardianconnect.icu.

8. Children

Guardian Connect is not directed at children under 16. Do not create an account if you are under 16; parents may add their own children as emergency contacts.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update this page and the date at the top. Material changes will be announced in the app.
