Questions, answered honestly

Is this a replacement for calling 911?

No — and we'll never pretend it is. Guardian Connect alerts the personal contacts you chose. It does not contact police, fire, or medical services. For life-threatening emergencies, call your local emergency number first. Think of Guardian Connect as the layer AROUND emergency services: the people who'd drop everything for you.

Who is it for?

Anyone whose safety net is people: students walking home, parents and their kids, elderly family members, lone workers, runners, travelers. If there are people who'd come for you, Guardian Connect connects you to them when it matters.

Does it track my location?

No. Your location is captured once, at the moment you trigger an emergency (or when you press "share my location" during one). There is no background tracking, no location history outside emergencies, and your location is only ever visible to the contacts in that emergency. See the Privacy Policy.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Guardian Connect is free. If it earns your trust, optional donations keep the lights on — but safety features will never be behind a paywall.

Can the alert fail?

Yes, and you should know that: alerts depend on phone batteries, signal, notification permissions, and your contacts being reachable. That's true of every app like this — we just say it out loud. Never rely on any app as your only safety measure.

Guardian Connect is not an emergency service and is not a replacement for 911 or your local emergency number.
